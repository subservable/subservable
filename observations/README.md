# Observations

Observations preserve noticed patterns, questions, and relationships before interpretation.

An observation is not a claim.

An observation does not assert truth.

An observation preserves context so that future claims may be evaluated without losing the original seam.

## Lifecycle

Observation:

\[
O
\]

may become:

\[
O \rightarrow C
\]

where:

\[
C
\]

is a claim supported by additional witnesses and evaluation.

## Principles

- Preserve before judging.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Keep uncertainty visible.
- Allow future reconstruction of why something was noticed.

An observation may remain an observation indefinitely.
