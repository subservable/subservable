## Reconstruction Test

A successful artifact should allow:

\[
R(\Delta(A)) \approx A
\]

Meaning:

- The reconstruction does not recreate the original atoms.
- It recreates the relationships.
- The glass is not the walk.
- The notation is not the conversation.
- The symbols are not the feeling.
- But together they preserve a path back.
- No information is invented.
- Only compressed.
