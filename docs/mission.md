# Mission

> **Mutable Interpretation. Immutable Reality.**

Subservable is founded on the belief that mathematics is a universal language of structure—not a barrier to understanding.

The project exists to make mathematical thinking more approachable through construction rather than memorization.

Rather than expecting mathematical fluency before contributing, Subservable encourages mathematical intuition by allowing the specification, implementation, and visualization to reinforce one another.

The system is designed so that building software also builds understanding.

---

# Canonical Grammar

The mathematical notation defined by the specification is the **canonical grammar** of Subservable.

It is the single semantic source from which every other representation is derived.

Those representations include:

- Executable notation (TypeScript)
- Visual grammar (diagrams, timelines, debugging views)
- Structural serialization (JSON and other interchange formats)
- Localized natural languages (English, Español, 日本語, Français, …)

No projection owns the meaning.

Every projection preserves the same immutable semantics.

Changing the representation **MUST NOT** change the meaning.

---

# Executable Notation

Executable notation is not an API.

Executable notation is the canonical grammar rendered as executable code.

The implementation should preserve a recognizable correspondence with the mathematical specification so that the specification and implementation can be understood together rather than translated between.

The implementation is therefore the first reader of the specification.

The goal is not to hide the mathematics.

The goal is to make the mathematics discoverable through implementation.

---

# Mathematics as a Portable Language

Subservable treats mathematics as a portable language.

A language should become easier through use.

Reading the specification should explain the implementation.

Reading the implementation should explain the mathematics.

Building the implementation should improve mathematical intuition.

The project intentionally creates a feedback loop between thinking, implementation, and understanding.

Over time, mathematical notation becomes less like specialized symbolism and more like a practical language for describing immutable relationships.

---

# Mental Mathematics

The mathematics used throughout Subservable is intentionally grounded in mental models before formalism.

The objective is not abstraction for its own sake.

The objective is clarity.

The notation should be understandable by reading it.

The implementation should reinforce that understanding.

The visual language should make those same relationships immediately recognizable.

Every projection teaches the same ideas through a different medium.

---

# Records as Reconstruction

Traditional software records describe state.

Subservable records describe reconstruction.

An Observation preserves what was observed.

A Procedure preserves how meaning was derived.

An Artifact preserves the resulting representation together with its associated uncertainty.

A Provenance record preserves how that derivation occurred.

Together they form an immutable sequence that can be replayed, inspected, visualized, and understood from multiple perspectives.

A traditional record resembles a photograph.

A Subservable record resembles a reconstructable film.

The observer may scrub through time, inspect intermediate transformations, replay derivations, or apply entirely different visual projections without altering the underlying history.

Meaning is reconstructed.

History remains immutable.

---

# The Three Axes

Subservable is intentionally developed along three inseparable axes.

## Philosophical

Knowledge is represented as immutable observations and reproducible derivations rather than assertions of absolute truth.

Interpretation may evolve.

Observations do not.

---

## Mathematical

The canonical grammar defines deterministic, composable, reproducible relationships.

Every implementation, visualization, serialization, and localization preserves those semantics.

Mathematics is the source language of the system.

---

## Educational

The implementation teaches the notation from which it is built.

Understanding grows through construction.

The system is designed so that learning mathematics becomes a natural consequence of building with it.

---

# Vision

Subservable is not merely a software framework.

It is an environment for observing, reconstructing, and understanding knowledge.

Its purpose is to preserve observations, expose derivations, encourage exploration, and make mathematical thinking more accessible through executable notation, visual reasoning, and immutable history.

The philosophy motivates the mathematics.

The mathematics defines the implementation.

The implementation teaches the mathematics.

Together they create a system that is simultaneously a specification, an implementation, and a learning environment.
