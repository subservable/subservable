# Claims

Claims are the primary unit of knowledge within Subservable.

Each claim is an immutable artifact that may be evaluated, replayed, refined, and related to other claims through provenance.

A claim consists of five documents:

- **claim.md** — the normative statement.
- **supporting-assets.md** — explanatory lenses, examples, diagrams, and teaching material.
- **discovery-process.md** — the replayable evolution of the claim.
- **witnesses.md** — supporting and contradicting evidence.
- **status.md** — current implementation and evaluation status.

## Design Principles

A claim should remain as small as possible.

Teaching belongs in supporting assets.

Evidence belongs in witnesses.

Exploration belongs in the discovery process.

Current confidence belongs in status.

Normative statements belong only in the claim itself.

A claim should preserve unknowns rather than prematurely resolving them.

New concepts should emerge through repeated replay before becoming system primitives.
