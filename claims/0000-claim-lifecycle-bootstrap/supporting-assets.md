# Supporting Assets

## Purpose

Supporting assets explain the purpose and role of this bootstrap claim.

They are instructional material.

They are not required for reconstruction.

---

## System Lens

The bootstrap claim represents the smallest useful demonstration of the Subservable lifecycle.

It answers:

> Can the system preserve something that claims to exist?

---

## Mathematical Lens

The reconstruction invariant:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R(\Delta(R)) = R
}
\]

means that replaying the derivation of a reconstructed artifact produces the same artifact.

---

## Bedrock Lens

This claim is a foundation layer.

It does not attempt to explain higher layers.

It only provides a stable surface for future claims.

---

## Teaching Lens

A small successful replay is more valuable than an untested large theory.

The bootstrap claim exists to make future claims testable.
